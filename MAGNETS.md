# Experimental dataset magnet links

This file preserves the exact magnet URIs used in the public BitTorrent distribution experiment reported in the CAA 2026 paper *The Jolly Roger showed the way: Decentralised archaeological data sharing using peer-to-peer and BitTorrent technologies*.

The dataset payloads are **not** stored in this repository. These magnet URIs identify the original torrents used in the experiment.

## Provenance

Both magnet links were publicly distributed by Juan Francisco Palomeque-Gonzalez during the 2026 proof-of-concept experiment. They are preserved here so the exact torrent identities used in the study remain part of the archived software record.

Availability through BitTorrent depends on at least one peer continuing to seed the corresponding payload. Preservation of the magnet URI or infohash does not itself guarantee continuing payload availability.
