# Experimental observations

This file documents the aggregate observations retained from the public BitTorrent proof-of-concept experiment reported in the CAA 2026 paper.

The machine-readable values are in [`experiment_observations.csv`](experiment_observations.csv). They are a transcription of the contemporaneous experiment notes and the fixed torrent configuration. Values that were not retained at the time of the experiment are explicitly marked as such rather than reconstructed retrospectively.

## Scope

The observations include dataset size and torrent settings, the initial seeding setup, approximately 24-hour swarm counts, the retained download-speed threshold observation, archive extraction verification, and aggregate Reddit Post Insights used in the paper.

No participant-level or personally identifying information is included.

## Limitations

Only the approximately 24-hour seeder counts were retained for the two torrents. No complete swarm growth time series, repeated throughput benchmark, or dataset-specific throughput distribution was preserved. The CSV therefore reports only observations supported by the original notes.
